A quick guide to the terms

Audit
A saved comparison of expected and observed settings.
Baseline
The expected settings used as a reference. The built-in sample has no recorded administrator approval.
Observed configuration
The settings found in a sample snapshot. This demo does not read a real device.
Drift / finding
Drift means a difference from the baseline. A finding explains a supported change and its risk.
SNMP, VLAN, route, ACL
SNMP location describes where a device is located. VLANs group network traffic. Routes direct traffic to its destination. ACLs are ordered rules that allow or block traffic; their order matters.
Draft patch
A text proposal for updating the baseline. It is never applied by this dashboard.
JSON record
A downloadable structured file containing the saved evidence or decision history.
